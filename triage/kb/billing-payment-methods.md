# Updating your payment method

Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method** to add or replace a card. Meridian
accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We also support ACH direct debit for
workspaces on an annual plan.

Adding a new card automatically makes it the default and removes the previous card
once the new one is verified with a small temporary authorization hold, which is
released within a few business days.

Only Owners and Billing Admins can change the payment method.

If your card was declined, update it here and then use **Retry payment** on the
outstanding invoice rather than waiting for the next automatic attempt.

We do not store full card numbers; payment details are held by our payment
processor and Meridian sees only the last four digits and expiry date.
