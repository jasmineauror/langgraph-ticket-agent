# Resetting your password

Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Enter your email and we send a reset
link valid for **60 minutes**. Requesting a new link immediately invalidates any
previous one.

For security we return the same confirmation message whether or not an account
exists for that address, so a successful-looking response does not by itself confirm
the account.

If the email does not arrive, check spam, then confirm you are using the exact
address your account was created with. Workspaces with SSO enforced do not use
Meridian passwords at all; reset your password with your identity provider instead.

Changing your password signs you out of all other sessions. Active API keys are not
affected by a password change and continue to work.

If you no longer have access to the email address on the account, an Owner of your
workspace can change it for you under member settings.
