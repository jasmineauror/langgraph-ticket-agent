# Finding and downloading invoices

All invoices for your Meridian workspace are available under
**Settings → Billing → Invoice history**. Invoices are generated on the first day of
each billing cycle and remain downloadable as PDFs indefinitely.

Only workspace members with the **Owner** or **Billing Admin** role can see the
invoice history. If the Billing tab is not visible to you, ask an Owner to grant you
the Billing Admin role.

Each invoice PDF includes your workspace name, the billing period, itemized seat
counts, and any applicable tax. If you need a specific purchase-order number or
company address to appear on the invoice, set it under **Settings → Billing →
Billing details** before the cycle closes; changes do not apply retroactively to
already-issued invoices.

Invoices are also emailed automatically to the billing contact address on each
cycle.
