# Two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA under **Settings → Profile → Two-factor authentication**. Meridian
supports TOTP authenticator apps such as 1Password, Authy, and Google Authenticator,
and hardware security keys via WebAuthn.

When you enable 2FA you are shown **ten single-use recovery codes**. Store them
somewhere other than the device holding your authenticator. Each code works once.

Owners and Admins can require 2FA for everyone in the workspace under **Settings →
Security → Require 2FA**. Members without it are prompted to set it up at their next
sign-in and cannot access the workspace until they do.

**Lost your device.** Use a recovery code to sign in, then remove the old
authenticator and enroll the new one. If you have neither the device nor a recovery
code, a workspace Owner can reset 2FA on your account. If you are the sole Owner in
that position, contact support; identity verification is required and takes several
business days.
