# Tax, VAT, and business tax IDs

Add your business tax ID under **Settings → Billing → Billing details → Tax ID**.
We support VAT numbers for EU workspaces, GST numbers for Australia, Canada, and
India, and US state sales-tax exemption certificates.

Once a valid EU VAT number is recorded, subsequent invoices are issued without VAT
under the reverse-charge mechanism. Validation happens against the official registry
and can take up to one business day.

Tax IDs apply only to invoices issued after the ID is validated. We cannot reissue
an already-finalized invoice to add a tax ID retroactively, so add it before your
next cycle closes.

If you hold a US sales-tax exemption, upload the certificate on the same page; a
member of the billing team reviews it manually.
