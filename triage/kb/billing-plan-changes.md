# Upgrading, downgrading, and proration

Change your plan under **Settings → Billing → Plan**.

**Upgrades** take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the
remainder of the current billing period, calculated per day on the difference
between the two plan prices.

**Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep
access to the higher-tier features until then. No partial credit is issued for the
unused portion of the period you already paid for.

Seat count changes work the same way: adding seats is prorated and charged
immediately, removing seats takes effect at the next cycle.

If a downgrade would put you below your current usage, for example fewer seats than
you have active members, the change is blocked until you remove the excess. The
Plan page shows exactly what needs to change before the downgrade can be scheduled.
