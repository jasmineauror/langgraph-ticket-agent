# Deleting your account or workspace

**Your own account.** Under **Settings → Profile → Delete account**. If you are the
sole Owner of a workspace you must transfer ownership or delete the workspace first.

**A whole workspace.** Owners can delete a workspace under **Settings → General →
Delete workspace**. You must type the workspace name to confirm.

Deletion is **not immediate**. The workspace enters a 30-day grace period during
which it is inaccessible to all members but fully restorable by contacting support.
This exists specifically so an accidental or malicious deletion can be undone.

After 30 days, raw event data, dashboards, and member records are permanently
purged from primary storage. Encrypted backups roll off within a further 60 days.

Deleting a workspace cancels its subscription. Any remaining prepaid period is
handled case by case and is not automatically credited.
